Quick Support

Before you order

Keep your event date, preferred style, size details, and budget range ready. That helps us guide you faster.

WhatsApp the studio View all channels
Do you offer custom sizing or fit guidance?

Yes. Many pieces can be adapted based on size, measurements, and the overall look you want. We confirm what is possible for each design before you place the order.

How do I place an order?

Start from the catalog, shortlist the pieces you like, and contact us over WhatsApp or email. We then confirm availability, pricing, sizing, timelines, and the next step for payment.

Are the discounts on the catalog final?

The catalog currently uses sample offer presentation so you can see how the original price, savings, and current price will appear. Final order pricing is always confirmed directly with the studio.

Can I visit the studio in person?

Yes. You can visit us at B27, Sarlabagh Extension, DayalBagh, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282005. Monday to Saturday timings are 10 am to 7:30 pm, and Sunday timings are 10 am to 5:30 pm.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timelines depend on whether the piece is ready inventory or needs customization. We share the expected timeline before you confirm the order.

Do you accept returns or exchanges?

Customized items are generally not returnable unless the delivered item is materially different from the confirmed order. Ready inventory queries are handled case by case after reviewing the product condition and the timing of the request.

Where can I see reviews or latest updates?

You can scan the QR codes on the Contact page, visit our Instagram handle @ruhani.creations, or search for Ruhani Creations by Sumati on Google Maps.
