How to Order

Terms & Conditions

Clear expectations around orders, customization, delivery, and support.

These terms explain how orders are discussed and confirmed through Ruhani Creations by Sumati across clothing, hand-painted accents, archive references, and future design categories. Last updated: March 19, 2026.

Orders

Subject to confirmation

Availability, final price, and customization scope are confirmed directly with the studio before any order is treated as accepted.

Payments

Advance may apply

Custom or made-to-order work may require an advance payment. Order processing starts only after payment confirmation.

Returns

Custom pieces are limited

Customized pieces are generally non-returnable except where the delivered product materially differs from the confirmed order.

1. Product display and catalog presentation

The website is intended to present current and recent styles, reference pricing, and brand information. Colours may vary slightly based on lighting, screen settings, or photography.

2. Availability and order acceptance

Products shown on the site are subject to availability at the time of inquiry. An item is not reserved until the studio confirms availability and the order details have been accepted directly with you.

3. Pricing and customization

Catalog prices may be revised depending on size changes, design changes, custom work, or add-on finishing. Sample discounts shown on the site are for catalog display planning and do not substitute a direct order confirmation.

4. Payments

Payment terms are shared directly during order confirmation. For custom work or specially prepared pieces, full or partial advance payment may be requested before work begins.

5. Delivery and timelines

Delivery timelines are estimates. Ready inventory, customization, transport conditions, and courier timelines can affect the final schedule. The studio is not responsible for delays caused by courier partners or circumstances outside reasonable control.

6. Returns, exchanges, and issue reporting

If you believe an item was delivered damaged or materially incorrect, notify us as soon as reasonably possible with photos and order context. Ready inventory concerns are reviewed case by case. Customized orders are generally non-returnable unless the studio confirms a clear issue with fulfillment.

7. Contact

For questions related to these terms, contact support@ruhanicreationsbysumati.com or message us on WhatsApp.
