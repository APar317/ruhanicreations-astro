About Us

A design-led creative studio shaped by emotion, hand-painting, and meaningful detail.

Ruhani Creations by Sumati is rooted in handmade beauty, where clothing, painted accents, and future lifestyle pieces all carry softness, refinement, and the intimacy of something thoughtfully created.

Founder Story Brand Philosophy Our Values

Founder

Sumati Aswani

I am Sumati Aswani, founder of Ruhani Creations, an artist with a deep love for beauty, detail, and meaningful expression. My journey began with hand-painting, a quiet and personal practice that gradually became a signature part of who I am and how I create.

What started as a passion for colour and brushwork evolved into a creative path shaped by emotion, refinement, and individuality. My work draws from the poetry of everyday moments, the softness of nature, and the elegance found in handmade artistry.

I create with the intention of bringing together sensitivity and sophistication, pieces that feel personal, timeless, and full of soul. For me, art is more than something to be seen; it is something to be felt. Each creation carries a story, a mood, and a piece of my creative spirit.

What defines Sumati’s approach

  • Painterly colour choices and delicate detailing
  • Design decisions guided by emotion, not only trend
  • A preference for pieces that feel elevated yet personal
  • Direct involvement in the creative and customer journey

Brand Story

Where handmade art becomes a wider design language.

The brand was born from the art of hand-painting and from a love of transforming simple surfaces into expressive, one-of-a-kind pieces. What began as a personal form of artistic exploration gradually blossomed into a creative studio rooted in craftsmanship, beauty, and intentional design.

At its heart, Ruhani Creations by Sumati celebrates the charm of handmade art with a refined and modern sensibility. Every piece is approached with care, inspired by emotion, detail, and the desire to create something truly distinctive, whether it becomes an outfit, a dupatta, a gifting piece, or a functional object.

This brand is an extension of Sumati’s artistic journey: a space to create meaningful work that feels both delicate and elevated, intimate and timeless.

What Guides Us

01

Artistry First

Hand-painted influence, colour sensitivity, and thoughtful finishing remain central to every collection and every styling decision.

02

Personal Expression

The goal is not to produce something generic. Each piece is intended to feel expressive, warm, and memorable for the person wearing it.

03
